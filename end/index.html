Tria left home.

He did not vanish from the world. He grew older, carried three tablets from coat to coat, received an email, found an old hard drive, and eventually returned to this address.

The site had kept the parts of him that passed through it. Some were memories he never typed. Some may have belonged to the shadow. The archive does not distinguish between them.

In the scan of My Home's Window, the bars still divide the sky. Between their pencil shadows is one sentence:

Your shoelace is untied.

Tria was wearing slippers.
