I CAN'T GET RID OF IT.

我摆脱不了它。

It moved by itself.

它自己搬走了。

But I found numbers it left behind:

06 28 00 19 36

Maybe they are not a date. Maybe they are coordinates. Maybe they are a password.

也许它们不是日期。也许是坐标，或者密码。
