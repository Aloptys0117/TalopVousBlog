COMPUTER ROOM CACHE

folder label: 2007 / actual date: unavailable

The corner machine still had a green line down its monitor. The power came on. Windows XP loaded.

Tria entered the address of his old homepage. The counter read 0001337. The page claimed it had last been updated on March 17, 2008.

Every entry he remembered was there. At the bottom was one sentence he had never written:

You left. I stayed.

Below it was an entry dated that day:

YOU CAME BACK.

Tria did not type. He held the power button until the monitor went dark.
