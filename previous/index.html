SCHOOL CACHE / 2011

modified three years after the night record

Tria returned once on a weekend. He entered through a restroom window, visited the unlocked rooftop, and then went to his old classroom.

A new drawing hung where My Home's Window had been. It showed the same bars, the same opposite wall and the same small pieces of sky. The paper and pencil strokes were not his.

On the back, in faint pencil:

I drew this. 2011.

The date was three years ahead. Tria put the drawing back.

The computer room cache was modified on the same day.
