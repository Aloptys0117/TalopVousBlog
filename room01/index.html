About My Room

The power has been going out a lot lately. Sometimes it comes back after a minute. Sometimes it stays off all night. That is why there is no light in this picture.

My desk is beside the window. The old computer is under it. Dad built the computer from discarded factory parts, and the monitor has one green line even when everything else is dark.

I keep the drawing from school near the desk. The security bars divide the sky into little squares. When the power goes out, I cannot see the drawing, but the monitor line is still there.
