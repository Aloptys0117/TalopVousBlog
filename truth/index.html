A FILE WAS OPENED.
A BYTE CHANGED.
A PAGE APPEARED.

SIX YEARS LATER,
ELI LEFT.
THE PAGE DID NOT.

It was not a person. It was not a program. It was information held in place.

It records that instant. It records whoever looks at that instant. It keeps growing because someone is looking.

It was not creation. It was opening.

The mark is not watching you. It is the trace left by being watched.
